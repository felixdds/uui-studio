# Where I Hope This Project Goes Next

[简体中文](../08-future.md) · English

I have used both Photoshop and PaintShop Pro for a long time. For some tasks, the latter fits my habits better and takes fewer clicks. But I would not insist that everyone choose the same tool I do.

For professional designers and programmers, years of accumulated skills, tools, and workflows are part of their work. It is not reasonable to expect them to abandon all that lightly.

## Respecting others and my own contribution

I do not expect every programmer to adopt UDM. Java, Rust, Python, and established frameworks all have their uses. My preferences and other people's choices can coexist.

AI has allowed me to participate in engineering work I would previously have struggled to do independently. I hope UUI Studio can further reduce repetitive work in cross-platform UI development, but that does not mean dismissing programmers' professional value.

"If AI can handle something, I try to let it" describes my division of work. I can still contribute judgments about requirements and architecture, and I hope to work with professional engineers in the same spirit of contributing our respective strengths.

## Why I hope to find collaborators

I am not good at promoting myself. If someone wants to learn about my experience, I share it. But sustaining a development platform also requires more actual use and engineering work.

I have considered teams such as ByteDance because I would like the platform to encounter different kinds of real application projects. OpenAI, or other organizations with relevant resources and interests, might also be possible parties to talk with. These are ideas for potential collaboration, not statements that a partnership or endorsement exists.

I hope a professional team can contribute to engineering, testing, documentation, promotion, and long-term maintenance, while I continue working on UDM's core architecture. When extending the system, I want us to understand why existing designs were chosen, then use discussion and actual results to decide what to retain and what to change.

A good architecture should not remain understandable only to the person who created it.

## There will still be things to explore in retirement

I hope to return to China after retirement and keep doing things that interest me. UUI Studio might become a product, or it might remain an interest I continue studying and improving for years.

I hope to clarify contributions to the work, third-party dependencies, and rights in future collaborations, so the project's development and my personal plans can fit together. I am in no hurry to implement every idea, and I do not treat the number of components as a score.

Can it make work easier? Can it remove unnecessary burdens? Can it develop reliably over the long term? Those are the questions I want to keep considering.

I do not know how far UUI Studio will ultimately go. But as long as I can look at a working system and ask, "Why couldn't this be a little better?", I will still have something that interests me.
