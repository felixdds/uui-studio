English

This policy applies to BMI Calculator by UUI Studio, including the Android application identified above. It describes the current offline calculator, including version 1.0.4.

Your height, weight and calculated BMI are processed on your device. The app does not send them to UUI Studio or to a third-party service. The app has no advertising, accounts or analytics/tracking SDKs.

1. Information used by the app

You may enter height and weight to calculate BMI and convert between metric and imperial units. These values and results are used locally for those functions. UUI Studio does not receive them through the app. The current Android build does not request Internet access and does not connect to a developer-operated server.

The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photographs or advertising identifier. It does not provide an account, cloud synchronization, or a saved BMI-history service.

2. Local storage and retention

The calculator initializes its input values when opened; Reset clears the current calculation. Android and the local runtime/WebView may maintain application files, preferences, caches and technical diagnostic logs on the device. These files are not automatically uploaded to UUI Studio.

You can remove local application data using Android Settings → Apps → BMI Calculator → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device). Uninstalling normally removes the app's local data. Any operating-system backup or restoration is governed by your Android/device settings and the relevant service provider, not by a UUI Studio cloud service.

3. Sharing, advertising and third parties

UUI Studio does not sell, rent or share your calculation inputs or results, and the app does not transmit them to us. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs. Android System WebView is used for local interface rendering.

Google Play, Android and device services may independently process installation, download, security or diagnostic information under their own policies and your settings. That is separate from data sent by this calculator.

4. This policy webpage

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it creates a web request to GitHub; GitHub may process technical website information such as IP addresses and request details under its privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, forms or tracking cookies to this page. Opening this policy does not send your BMI inputs or result to the website.

5. Questions and voluntary contact

For a privacy question, use the developer contact shown on the BMI Calculator Google Play listing or contact UUI Studio through the project's support issues. GitHub issues are public: do not post health information, calculation values, private logs or other sensitive information there. If you voluntarily send a support message, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to address your request; the communication service also applies its own policies.

6. Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. New features that transmit or collect data require corresponding disclosures. This policy describes the current app and does not promise future features.

BMI Calculator 隐私政策

开发者：UUI Studio。Android 包名：org.uui.bmicalculator。生效及更新日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日。本政策适用于当前离线 BMI 计算器，包括 1.0.4 版本。

身高、体重及 BMI 结果在您的设备上处理，应用不会将它们发送给 UUI Studio 或第三方服务。应用没有广告、账号或分析追踪 SDK。

1. 应用使用的信息

您可以输入身高、体重，进行 BMI 计算及公制/英制单位换算。这些数值与结果仅在本机用于上述功能，UUI Studio 不会通过本应用收到这些信息。当前 Android 构建未申请联网权限，也不连接开发者运营的服务器。

应用不要求您提供姓名、电子邮箱、通讯录、位置、相机、麦克风、照片或广告标识符；不提供账号、云同步或 BMI 历史保存服务。

2. 本地存储与保留

计算器打开时初始化输入，Reset（重置）清除当前计算。Android 及本地运行环境/WebView 可能在设备上维护应用文件、偏好设置、缓存和技术诊断日志。这些文件不会自动上传给 UUI Studio。

您可以通过 Android“设置 → 应用 → BMI Calculator → 存储 → 清除存储”删除本地应用数据，具体文字因设备而异。卸载通常会移除本地应用数据。操作系统的备份和恢复由您的 Android/设备设置及相应服务方管理，并非 UUI Studio 提供的云服务。

3. 共享、广告与第三方

UUI Studio 不出售、出租或共享您的计算输入或结果，应用也不会把它们传给我们。应用未包含广告或第三方分析追踪 SDK；Android System WebView 用于渲染本地界面。

Google Play、Android 和设备服务可能依据各自政策及您的设置，独立处理安装、免费下载、安全或诊断信息。这与本计算器主动发送数据是不同的事项。

4. 本政策网页

本页由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问页面会向 GitHub 发出网络请求，GitHub 可能依据其隐私声明处理 IP 地址、请求详情等网站技术信息。我们未在本页加入分析、广告脚本、表单或追踪 Cookie。打开本政策不会将您的 BMI 输入或结果发送给网站。

5. 问题与主动联系

如有隐私问题，可使用 BMI Calculator Google Play 详情页所列的开发者联系方式，或通过项目支持 Issues联系 UUI Studio。GitHub Issues 是公开的，请勿在那里提交健康信息、计算数值、私密日志或其他敏感内容。您主动发送支持消息时，我们会收到您选择提供的信息，并用于处理您的请求；通信服务本身也适用其政策。

6. 政策更新

如应用的数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策和日期。新增传输或收集数据的功能需要相应披露。本政策描述当前应用，不承诺未来功能。
