## What it shows

Clearly labeled sample translation entries demonstrate the removed, pending, and updated groups, regrouping after saving, further editing of completed translations, and confirmed cleanup.

The default task policy is used for a BMI title change, a build, and an actual Reset check. The staged workflow then separates the title change from building and checking. Studio genuinely pauses before stage two and requires approval of the current stage. The recording ends with native approval records.

## Recording and scope

The recording uses an isolated copy of the latest published payload and the real OpenAI service. Project-source hashes remain unchanged before approval, and subsequent stages have separate native approval records. No model messages, approval cards, or check results were fabricated. Acceleration and extended reading holds are labeled in both languages. A take where the model merely claimed to pause without reaching native approval was excluded; the final pause comes from an actual tool request.

Coverage is limited to these Windows, OpenAI, single-Form scenarios. It does not establish full acceptance of every platform, component, or model. Software packages and their checksums are unchanged. The file is prepared for subsequent upload to YouTube; no YouTube upload was performed here.
